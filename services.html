Core Capabilities

Form, quiet rhythm, and honest masonry.

We specialize in private residential architecture, deep spatial renovations, and custom millwork programs that prioritize tactile comfort and structural clarity over temporary trends.

Phase I — III

Architectural Design

Full-service spatial planning from initial concept schematics to building department approvals. We focus on natural light path studies, contextual site alignment, and silent exterior envelopes that transition smoothly into interior spaces.

Details & Millwork

Interior Architecture

Developing the tactile baseline of your daily life. This includes hand-drawn custom cabinetry, integrated stone basins, custom plumbing specification, and a meticulously curated lighting layout to enhance plaster finishes.

Sourcing & Curation

Material Curation

Sourcing textured wall coatings, salvaged timber, hand-made clay bricks, and mineral finishes. We establish a restricted physical board of textures at the project start, ensuring cohesive flow through every threshold.

The Studio Method

A slow, deliberate build from hand-sketch to hand-over.

We work with a select handful of active commissions annually. This ensures that every foundation poured, layout revised, and custom metal pull commissioned receives rigorous attention.

01 / Dialogue

We gather for an initial consult on-site. We listen closely to the light behavior, the surrounding flora, and your physical rhythms to frame the preliminary floorplan.

02 / Documentation

Our studio translates initial ideas into extensive construction packages. Every material transition, cabinet joint, and floor threshold is drafted explicitly.

03 / Refinement

We review actual tactile samples of lime plaster, seasoned timbers, and honed limestone in the studio under varied light conditions before final commitment.

04 / Supervision

We maintain close relationships with elite local timberwrights and stonemasons, conducting frequent site visits to ensure absolute precision.
