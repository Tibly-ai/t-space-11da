Detailed Architectural Commissions

A breakdown of four foundational projects exploring private courtyards, natural topography, and historical preservation.

Project 01 / Kitchen & Hearth

The Timber Pavilion

Constructed alongside an established oak woodland, this dwelling features custom raw Douglas fir carpentry designed to mirror the structural trunk spacing of the surrounding forest canopy.

  • MaterialsDouglas Fir, Travertine, Raw Plaster
  • ScopeFull Architecture & Interior Joinery
Project 02 / Coastal Reticence

Bay Sand House

Perched on a quiet granite shelf facing the tidal marshlands. The building sits low in the landscape, protected by a continuous deep cedar shingle skin that weather-burnishes into a silver-grey coat.

  • MaterialsAlaskan Yellow Cedar, Board-Formed Concrete
  • ScopeSite Integration, Building Envelope
Project 03 / Urban Infill

The Courtyard Cloister

An inward-looking residence built on a narrow historic lot. Traditional raw lime render and structural concrete columns enclose a central private garden space, keeping urban sounds entirely outside.

  • MaterialsLimewash, Poured Concrete, River Stone
  • ScopeFull Plan, Structural Engineering
